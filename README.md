# Operation Crimson Scorpion Simulator

A browser-based trainer for the Operation CRIMSON SCORPION (OCS) tabletop wargame, built for Mod 2 wargaming familiarization.

**Play:** https://zacharysa11.github.io/crimson-scorpion/

Works on phones and laptops, no sign-in needed:

- Guided tutorial, drills, rules check and playbook
- Play the computer (three difficulty levels, with a coach)
- Play a classmate by turn code or pass and play
- On-screen d4 dice, or enter your real dice
- On phones, a card tray (Squadrons / Enablers buttons) shows your cards without covering the board
- Full rules reference and every card and token from the real box: per side 5 missions, 5 postures, 10 squadrons and 34 enablers, with 34 US and 33 PRC tokens
- The real board layout, and every card effect played out, including response cards (Counter Space, Resilient Bases, Air Launched Decoy, Reserves and the rest)

Adapted for training from the OCS User Guide (17 FEB 26) and the Activation and Move-Acquire-Shoot tutorial. Card and token values are transcribed from the printed set; the cards and board are photographs of the printed set (straightened and cropped); the tokens are drawn from it. Where a card's wording leaves room, the simulator's reading is listed in Rules → How this simulator plays it.
