# Operation Crimson Scorpion Simulator

A browser-based trainer for the Operation CRIMSON SCORPION (OCS) tabletop wargame, built for Mod 2 wargaming familiarization.

**Play:** https://zacharysa11.github.io/crimson-scorpion/

Works on phones and laptops, no sign-in needed:

- Guided tutorial, drills, rules check and playbook
- Play the computer (three difficulty levels, with a coach)
- Play a classmate by turn code or pass and play
- On-screen d4 dice, or enter your real dice
- Full rules reference and every card and token

Adapted for training from the OCS User Guide (17 FEB 26). Cards and tokens not shown in the guide are simulator estimates; use your physical cards where they differ.
